NASA Challenge: Abandoned But Not Forgotten

Since the 1960s, NASA has left unique scientific hardware across our solar system—on the Moon, on Mars, and in deep space. From rovers that explored alien plains to probes still traveling out past the planets, this hardware varies widely in its purpose and functionality. This terminal introduces school-age space enthusiasts to these incredible machines and the groundbreaking science discoveries they left behind for humanity.
