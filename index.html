Introduction: Beyond Space Junk

Humanity builds monuments out of stone to remember our history on Earth. But across the cosmos, our first interplanetary monuments are made of titanium, solar panels, and gold foil. These machines are not space garbage. They are our silent pioneers—the lone vanguards of human curiosity left resting on the shores of alien worlds.
