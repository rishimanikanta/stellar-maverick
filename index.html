Introduction: The Challenge Objective

Since the dawn of the 1960s space race, NASA has deployed complex scientific hardware across the vast expanses of our solar system—permanently leaving landing structures, rovers, and tracking modules resting on the Moon, the surface of Mars, and deep cosmic space. While some hardware systems are completely silent monuments, others are robotic probes still actively traveling away from Earth into interstellar territory. These devices vary drastically in their design purpose, construction, and current level of functionality. This application serves as an interactive historical repository designed to introduce school-age space enthusiasts to these incredible engineering feats, uncovering the structural anatomy of the hardware configurations and the revolutionary scientific space discoveries they made possible.
